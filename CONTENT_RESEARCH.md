## 2. Core Heritage & Brand Narrative

### 2.1 The 1965 Origin
Before becoming a favourite multi-generational dining destination, Hotel Sunny Point began in 1965 as a modest local eatery situated where the current customer parking lot exists today. It provided authentic, freshly prepared, homely meals to the residents and travellers of Bhadrawati.

### 2.2 The 1995 South Indian Revolution
In 1995, Hotel Sunny Point expanded its culinary horizons by introducing authentic South Indian cuisine, becoming one of the pioneer establishments to bring crispy fermented dosas, idlis, and vadas to the town of Bhadrawati. Its signature golden Masala Dosa accompanied by coconut chutney and sambar quickly evolved into a beloved household tradition across three generations.

### 2.3 Evolution into "Global Bites"
Today, operating from a modern three-story building in Bhadrawati, Hotel Sunny Point offers a comprehensive pure vegetarian culinary experience spanning South Indian, North Indian/Punjabi, Indo-Chinese, Continental, Italian, and Mexican cuisines, while maintaining strict adherence to pure vegetarian authenticity.
